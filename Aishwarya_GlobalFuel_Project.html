Project overview

From raw data to clear insights

The project summarizes weekly fuel price data into visual comparisons designed to help students, analysts and policy readers explore patterns quickly.

The problem

A large weekly dataset is difficult to scan manually. Price gaps between countries, regional tax patterns, subsidy differences and movements alongside crude oil are not immediately obvious in a spreadsheet.

The approach

Tableau worksheets combine averages into a single dashboard. Viewers can compare countries and explore trends using map, ranking, time-series and category charts.

What the dashboard explores

  • ✓Average petrol prices on a world map
  • ✓Top 10 most expensive countries
  • ✓Petrol and diesel trends over time
  • ✓Petrol price compared with Brent crude
  • ✓Tax share by region and prices by income/subsidy level
